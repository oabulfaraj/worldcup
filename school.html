How to subscribe on iPhone
iPhone / iPad
  1. Tap the gold “Subscribe now” button above.
  2. iPhone asks “Subscribe to this calendar?” — tap Subscribe.
  3. Pick a calendar and tap Add.
  4. Done! All school dates appear and stay up to date.
Android / Google Calendar
Android
  1. Tap “Copy subscription link” above.
  2. Open calendar.google.com in a browser.
  3. Settings ⚙️ → “Add calendar” → “From URL”.
  4. Paste the link and tap Add.

Note: Some holiday dates (Ramadan, Eid) are tentative. The Ministry of Education has not yet released the official academic calendar, so dates may change based on the MOE’s final announcement. Any update will reach subscribers automatically.
